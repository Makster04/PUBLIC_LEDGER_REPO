# INDEX DESIGNS

The full design of each index, with the keys and free sources it uses.

**How the math works for all of them**
- **Turning a part into 0–100:** a share is multiplied by 100, so 30% becomes 30. Anything else is compared with a ceiling. At or above the ceiling counts as 100, and below it scales down. For example, with a ceiling of 20%, a share of 8% scores 40.
- **The index:** the weighted average of its parts.
- **Missing data:** a missing part drops out and the other parts are re-weighted. If a part marked *required* is missing, the index shows "Not enough data".
- **Party comparison:** every index also shows how the person compares with the average for their party and chamber, for example "+12 vs. party."
- **Reviewed data only:** refreshed records stay as leads until they're reviewed.
- **One calculation per index:** each index is calculated once, on its home tab. If another tab shows it, it's the same number with a link back.
- **Keys:** in this section, [x] after a key means you already have it. "No key" sources work for free.

## ETHICS SCORE ⚖️

### Legal Trouble Index 🚨
**Measures:** how serious their legal and ethics cases are, from official records.

Each case is worth its **status points × kind of case × age**:

- **Status points:**
  - Confirmed finding: 20 (a conviction, guilty plea, ethics committee finding, agency penalty, or the person admitting it).
  - Open investigation: 15 (an indictment not yet resolved, an active probe or a pending lawsuit).
  - Closed without a finding: 5 (acquittal, dismissal, no charges, or a settlement without admitting anything).
  - Allegation: 0. Listed, but no official body acted on it.
- **Kind of case:**
  - Criminal: × 1.5 (indictments, convictions, guilty pleas).
  - Ethics or civil: × 1 (House or Senate Ethics, agency enforcement, civil lawsuits).
  - Technical: × 0.5 (FEC fines for late reports, disclosure mistakes that were corrected).
- **Age:** × 0.5 if the case's latest status change was more than 10 years ago. Open cases always count in full.

The index is the total of every case's points, with a ceiling of 100.

| Status | Criminal | Ethics or civil | Technical |
|---|---|---|---|
| Confirmed finding | 30 | 20 | 10 |
| Open investigation | 22.5 | 15 | 7.5 |
| Closed without a finding | 7.5 | 5 | 2.5 |

**Rules:**
- **Each case counts once, at its current status.** When an indictment ends in a conviction, the entry is updated, not added again.
- **One matter is one case.** If the same conduct leads to both a DOJ case and an ethics probe, only the more serious one counts.
- **Leads don't count.** Court records, DOJ press releases and FEC enforcement records from the daily refresh count only after their status is confirmed from an official record.
- **No cases** scores 0, shown as "No recorded cases".

**Example:** an open criminal investigation (15 × 1.5 = 22.5), an ethics finding from 12 years ago (20 × 1 × 0.5 = 10) and a closed FEC late-filing case (5 × 0.5 = 2.5) give 22.5 + 10 + 2.5 = **35**.

**Keys and sources:**
- **The cases themselves:** the reviewed research. No key.
- **New leads to review:**
  - `COURTLISTENER_API_TOKEN`: court records.
  - `FEC_API_KEY` [x]: FEC enforcement records.
  - DOJ press releases: no key.

### Insider Trading Index 📈
**Measures:** stock trades that overlap with the person's power, donors or votes.

- **Committee conflicts (40%, required):** the dollar value of trades in single companies' stock, in sectors their committees oversee, ÷ the dollar value of all their single-company trades. Each trade's dollar value is the middle of its disclosed range. For example, "$1,001–$15,000" counts as $8,000.
- **Trades near related votes (25%):** the share of trades made within 30 days before or after a vote or bill action of theirs that affects that company's sector.
- **Trades in donors (20%):** the share of trades in a company whose PAC or employees gave to them, or that lobbied on a bill they acted on.
- **Single-stock activity (15%):** single-company trades per year, with a ceiling of 50 trades a year. Funds, ETFs and bonds don't count.

**Rules:**
- Spouse and dependent-child trades count and are labeled.
- Fewer than 5 reviewed trades means "Not enough data", with the raw flags still listed.
- No trades at all scores 0, shown as "No trades".
- People not in Congress show "No trading data".
- Trades that made unusually good returns are shown as leads only, never scored.

**Also shown on:** the Money & Influence tab, beside the trade list. It's the same number, not a second score.

**Example:** conflicts 30, near votes 20, donors 10, activity 24 gives (0.4×30) + (0.25×20) + (0.2×10) + (0.15×24) = **23**.

**Keys and sources:**
- **Trades:**
  - The members' own trade reports, from the House Clerk and Senate eFD: no key.
  - Cross-checks: `BARGO_API_KEY`, `EULERPOOL_API_KEY` and `FMP_API_KEY`, all optional.
- **Company sectors:** `SEC_USER_AGENT`, your name and email (not a key).
- **Committees and the sectors they oversee:** congress-legislators and the Senate and House committee lists. No key.
- **Votes and bill actions:**
  - Senate and House roll calls: no key.
  - `CONGRESS_API_KEY`: bill actions and House votes from 2023 on.
- **Donors:** `FEC_API_KEY` [x] for PAC money and donors by employer.
- **Lobbying:** `LDA_API_KEY` (optional).

### Stock Disclosure Dodger Index 🙈
**Measures:** stock and holdings reports that were filed late or never filed.

- **Late or missing trade reports (70%, required):** the share of trades reported after the 45-day deadline, or never reported.
- **Late annual financial disclosures (30%):** the share of the last 3 years' annual reports filed after the deadline, extensions included. These are the yearly reports that list holdings, including stocks. They come from the House disclosure index.

**Rules:**
- **Minimum:** at least 3 required filings.
- **People not in Congress:** "No trading data."
- **Late FEC campaign reports:** not part of this index, because they're campaign money, not stock. An FEC fine for late reports counts in the Legal Trouble Index 🚨 as a technical case once it's reviewed.

Late trade reports are counted here, not in the Insider Trading Index, so nothing is counted twice.

**Keys and sources:**
- **Trade and filing dates:** the members' own trade reports, from the House Clerk and Senate eFD (no key). The trade services above are optional cross-checks.
- **Annual disclosures:** the House disclosure index. No key.
- **Senators:** their trade reports are read from Senate eFD, but their annual disclosures aren't imported yet. For senators, the annual-disclosure part drops out and only the trade-report part counts.

### ↓↓↓

### New Ethics grade formula ⚖️
**Ethics** = 100 − Legal Trouble Index − (Insider Trading Index × 0.10) − (Stock Disclosure Dodger Index × 0.10). It never goes below 0.

- **Cases count in full.** They're the main part of the grade, and serious enough cases alone can bring it to 0.
- **Trading can cost at most 20 points,** the same as today. The deduction now reflects how serious the trading is, not just how many bad trades there were.

**Example:** Legal Trouble Index 35, Insider Trading Index 23 and Stock Disclosure Dodger Index 40 give 100 − 35 − 2.3 − 4 = **58.7, rounded to 59 (F)**.

The three indexes don't overlap:
- **Legal Trouble Index 🚨:** official findings, investigations and charges.
- **Insider Trading Index 📈:** what they traded, and whether it overlaps with their committees, votes or donors.
- **Stock Disclosure Dodger Index 🙈:** whether they reported it on time.

## WORKERS > PROFIT SCORE 👷

### Corporation Compromised Index 🏢
**Measures:** how much of their money comes from corporations, and how often they side with those funders.

- **Corporate PAC share (35%, required):** money from corporate and industry-group PACs ÷ campaign funds raised, over the same reports. The ceiling is 20%.
- **Corporate employees' giving (10%):** giving from employees of the companies among their top 20 donor organizations ÷ campaign funds raised. The ceiling is 10%.
- **Voting with business funders (35%):** on key votes where a business funder took a public side, how often they voted that way, minus their party's average on the same votes. Being at or below the party average scores 0, and the ceiling is 25 points above it. Business funders include the Chamber of Commerce, NFIB, Americans for Prosperity and Club for Growth.
- **Profit side on lobbied bills (20%):** on Workers > Profit bills that a top donor organization lobbied on, the share where they took the Profit side. This part needs at least 3 such bills.

Outside spending is left out, because candidates legally can't control it.

**Example:** PAC share 8% scores 40, employees 3% scores 30, +10 vs. party scores 40, and 2 of 4 lobbied bills scores 50. That gives (0.35×40) + (0.10×30) + (0.35×40) + (0.20×50) = **41**.

**Keys and sources:**
- **Campaign money:** `FEC_API_KEY` [x] for campaign totals, PAC contributions and donors by employer. PAC sectors are assigned by this app from committee names, with no key.
- **Business funders' positions:** the reviewed research (their scorecards and key-vote letters). No key.
- **Votes:** Senate and House roll calls (no key), and `CONGRESS_API_KEY` for House votes from 2023 on.
- **Lobbying:** `LDA_API_KEY` (optional).
- **Bills marked Profit or People:** the reviewed research. No key.

### Worker Backed Index ✊
The same formula with unions:
- union PAC share;
- union employees' giving (people employed by the unions themselves);
- voting with union funders such as the AFL-CIO, SEIU and AFSCME, compared with their party's average;
- the People side on bills that unions lobbied on.

It's shown next to the Corporation Compromised Index, so both sides are measured the same way.

**Keys and sources:** the same as the Corporation Compromised Index 🏢: `FEC_API_KEY` [x], `CONGRESS_API_KEY`, `LDA_API_KEY` (optional), the roll calls (no key) and the reviewed research.

### Grassroots Index 🌱
**Measures:** the share of campaign funds from donors who gave $200 or less, × 100. A score of 42 means 42% came from small donors.

**Minimum:** an FEC report.

**Keys and sources:** `FEC_API_KEY` [x], from the campaign totals (the FEC's "unitemized" contributions).

### ↓↓↓

### New Workers > Profit grade formula 👷
**Workers > Profit** = 75% from today's score (actions on marked bills) + 25% from (100 − Corporation Compromised Index).

- The grade still needs today's minimum number of scored actions on marked bills. Without them, it stays "Not graded", and the index is shown on its own.
- The Worker Backed ✊ and Grassroots 🌱 indexes sit beside the grade, not inside it.

## PROMISES DELIVERED SCORE 📦

### Flip-Flop Index 🩴
**Measures:** reversals over time in what they actually did.

- **Record switches (100%, required):** of the issues where they have recorded actions at least 2 years apart, the share where later actions took the opposite side from earlier ones. Recorded actions are votes, bills and official acts. For a president, that means signings, vetoes and executive orders.
- **Changed-position checks:** shown beside the index, not counted in it. They already count at half credit in the promise score.

**Minimum:** 5 issues with 2 or more actions each.

**Example:** 10 issues with actions at least 2 years apart, and on 3 of them later actions took the other side. That gives 3 ÷ 10 = **30**.

**Keys and sources:**
- **Recorded actions and how they're coded by issue:** the reviewed research. No key.
- **Newer votes:** Senate and House roll calls (no key), and `CONGRESS_API_KEY` for bills and House votes from 2023 on.
- **State legislators:** `OPENSTATES_API_KEY` and `LEGISCAN_API_KEY`.
- **A president's executive orders:** the Federal Register. No key.

### Pants on Fire Index 🔥
**Measures:** the average falseness of their fact-checked claims from the last 6 years.

- **Scoring each rating:** True 0, Mostly True 20, Half True 50, Mostly False 80, False 100, Pants on Fire 100.

**Minimum:** 10 ratings.

**Example:** 10 ratings: 2 Mostly True, 3 Half True, 3 Mostly False and 2 False. That gives (2×20 + 3×50 + 3×80 + 2×100) ÷ 10 = **63**.

**Keys and sources:** 
- `GOOGLE_API_KEY`, through Google's Fact Check Tools. It covers PolitiFact, FactCheck.org and other fact-checkers.

### ↓↓↓

### New Promises Delivered grade formula 📦
**Promises Delivered** = Promise score − Flip-Flop deduction − Pants on Fire deduction. It never goes below 0.

- **Promise score:** today's score, unchanged: (kept + ½ × partly kept or changed) ÷ counted checks. For a president, it's PolitiFact's promise trackers instead.
- **Flip-Flop deduction:** Flip-Flop Index × 0.10. At most 10 points.
- **Pants on Fire deduction:** (Pants on Fire Index − 50) × 0.20, only when the index is above 50. At most 10 points. Fact-checkers mostly pick claims that already sound doubtful, so only a mostly-false record costs points.
- The grade still needs today's minimum of 3 counted checks. The indexes adjust a grade but can't create one.
- An index showing "Not enough data" deducts nothing.
- Together the two indexes can take off at most 20 points, the same as trading on the Ethics grade.

**Example:** promise score 80, Flip-Flop Index 30 and Pants on Fire Index 63 give 80 − (30 × 0.10) − ((63 − 50) × 0.20) = 80 − 3 − 2.6 = **74.4, rounded to 74 (C)**.

**Option:** to keep Pants on Fire out of the grade, delete its deduction line and add "The Pants on Fire 🔥 index sits beside the grade, not inside it." The formula then becomes Promise score − Flip-Flop deduction.

## GET THINGS DONE SCORE 🏗️ (still optional fifth grade)
**Measures:** how well they do the job, whatever they stand for. It's shown as a fifth grade on each home card, with its own profile tab and its own page in the Scores menu.

### Get It Done Index ✅
**Measures:** how far their substantive bills get in the current Congress. Ceremonial and naming resolutions don't count.

- **Points per bill, by the furthest stage reached:**
  - committee action: 1
  - passed committee: 2
  - passed one chamber: 4
  - passed both chambers: 6
  - became law: 10
- **Ceiling:** 30 points per Congress for members of the majority party, and 15 for the minority. Minority-party bills rarely move, so they're compared on a fairer scale.

**Example:** a minority-party member with 3 bills that got committee action (3), 1 that passed committee (2) and 1 that passed the House (4) has 9 points ÷ 15 = **60**.

**Keys and sources:**
- **Their bills and how far each got:** `CONGRESS_API_KEY`, from their sponsored bills and each bill's actions.
- **Majority or minority party:** congress-legislators. No key.

### No-Show Index 🪑
**Measures:** missed roll calls ÷ the roll calls they were eligible for in the current Congress.

- **Ceiling:** 20% missed.
- **Medical leave:** votes missed during publicly announced medical leave don't count as missed.
- **Campaigning:** votes missed while running for another office count, and are labeled.

**Example:** 2% of votes missed ÷ the 20% ceiling = **10**.

**Keys and sources:**
- **Every roll call in the current Congress:** Senate.gov and the House Clerk (no key), and `CONGRESS_API_KEY` for House votes. The refresh reads only selected and recent votes today, so this needs a new job that reads them all.
- **Medical leave:** the member's own announcements and the news, in the reviewed research. No key.

### Across the Aisle Index 🌉
**Measures:** how much they work with the other party, in the current Congress.

- **Other-party cosponsors on their bills (50%):** the share of cosponsors on their bills who are from the other party.
- **Other-party bills they cosponsor (50%):** the share of the bills they cosponsored that were sponsored by the other party.

- **Ceiling:** 50% for each part.
- **Minimum:** 5 of their own bills with cosponsors.

**Example:** 20% other-party cosponsors (scores 40) and 20% other-party bills (scores 40) give **40**.

**Keys and sources:**
- **Bills, cosponsors and sponsors:** `CONGRESS_API_KEY`.
- **Each member's party:** congress-legislators. No key.

### Party Rebel Index 🤠
**Measures:** 100 − their party unity score (Voteview, 119th Congress).

- **Ceiling:** 20.
- **Also shown:** the number of key votes where they went against their party's majority.
- **Beside the grade, not inside it:** breaking from your party isn't good or bad in itself.

**Keys and sources:**
- **Party unity:** Voteview data. No key.
- **Key votes against their party:** Senate and House roll calls. No key.

### ↓↓↓

### New Get Things Done grade formula 🏗️
**Get Things Done** = (0.5 × Get It Done Index) + (0.3 × (100 − No-Show Index)) + (0.2 × Across the Aisle Index).

- **Letter grades:** the same scale as the other four grades. A is 90–100, down to F below 60.
- **Needs:** a congressional record in the current Congress, with a Get It Done Index. Everyone else, including most challengers and state officials, is "Not graded".
- **Missing parts:** if No-Show or Across the Aisle shows "Not enough data", the other parts are re-weighted.
- **Doesn't overlap the other grades:** none of the other four measure job performance. Your match stays about positions only.
- **Party Rebel 🤠** sits beside the grade, not inside it.

**Example:** Get It Done 60, No-Show 10 (so showing up scores 90) and Across the Aisle 40 give (0.5 × 60) + (0.3 × 90) + (0.2 × 40) = 30 + 27 + 8 = **65 (D)**.

## MONEY & INFLUENCE 💰
These stay beside the grades and carry the **Correlation, not cause** label.

### Stock trades (from the Ethics tab) 📈
Not a separate index:
- **What's shown:** the Insider Trading Index number from the Ethics tab, beside the existing "Stock holdings and trades" list and the "Stock in donors" stat.
- **Breakdown:** a link to the Ethics tab, which has the parts and the math.
- **Nothing calculated here:** the trade list and its donor flags stay context only. They feed the "Trades in donors" part of the Insider Trading Index, so the two tabs always agree.

**Keys and sources:** the same as the Insider Trading Index 📈 on the Ethics tab.

### Donor Loyalty Index 🤝
**Measures:** how much more they vote with their funders than members of their own party do.

1. For each funder that took public positions, take the person's rate of voting its way minus their party's average on those same votes.
2. Weight each funder by the money it gave or spent for them.
3. Only the amount above the party average counts. The ceiling is 25 points above.

**Minimum:** 5 compared votes.

**Keys and sources:**
- **Money from each funder:** `FEC_API_KEY` [x] for contributions and outside spending, plus the FEC's bulk files for earlier years (no key).
- **Funders' positions:** the reviewed research (their scorecards and key-vote letters). No key.
- **Votes:** Senate and House roll calls (no key), and `CONGRESS_API_KEY` for House votes from 2023 on.

### Super PAC Dependence Index 💸
**Measures:** super PAC spending for them ÷ (campaign funds raised + all outside spending for them), × 100.

- **Dates:** Jan. 1, 2025 – Sept. 30, 2026. The Q3 reports are in, so both sides now cover the same dates.
- **Covers:** general-election spending only.
- **Not shown:** for three-way races or races marked incomplete.

This replaces the withdrawn super PAC share.

**Keys and sources:** `FEC_API_KEY` [x], for campaign totals and independent expenditures.

### Lobbyist Overlap Index 🏛️
**Measures:** bills they sponsored, cosponsored or voted on that a top 20 donor organization lobbied on ÷ all bills they acted on in the same period.

- **Ceiling:** 20%.
- **Exposure only:** lobbying reports don't say which side the lobbyist wanted.

**Keys and sources:**
- **Lobbying reports:** `LDA_API_KEY` (optional).
- **Top donor organizations:** `FEC_API_KEY` [x].
- **Their bills:** `CONGRESS_API_KEY`.
- **Their votes:** Senate and House roll calls. No key.

### Contracts for Donors Index 📝
**Measures:** the number of their top 20 donor organizations that got federal contracts from agencies their committees oversee, ÷ 20, × 100. The total contract dollars are shown beside it.

**Needs:** a small new table matching agencies to the committees that oversee them.

**Keys and sources:**
- **Top donor organizations:** `FEC_API_KEY` [x].
- **Companies' federal IDs:** `SAM_API_KEY` [x].
- **Federal contracts:** USAspending. No key.
- **Their committees:** congress-legislators. No key.

### Foreign Agent Flag 🚩
A flag, not an index:
- **Yes or no:** whether any top donor organization, or a lobbyist working for one, is registered as a foreign agent (FARA).
- **Details:** each registration is listed.

**Keys and sources:**
- **Foreign-agent registrations:** FARA. No key.
- **Top donor organizations:** `FEC_API_KEY` [x].
- **Their lobbyists:** `LDA_API_KEY` (optional).
