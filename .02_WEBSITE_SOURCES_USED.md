# These websites are all free

## How fast they update, and what to change
- **How fast checks run:** websites share the same batches as the APIs. On your Mac, up to 40 pages a minute across all sources together. On Cloudflare, 8 pages every five minutes.
- **Pace:** each website's own cap per minute, kept low so the official sites aren't overloaded.
- **Nothing to renew:** none of these needs a key. Senate eFD asks every visitor to agree to its terms, and the refresh agrees at the start of each session.

| Website | Checked | Pace (pages a minute) | Key to change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Senate.gov (votes, committee lists) | Daily for new votes. Each vote is read once. | 10 | None |
| House Clerk (votes, committee lists) | Daily for new votes. Each vote is read once. | 10 | None |
| House Clerk disclosures (trade reports, filing index) | Daily | 10 | None |
| Senate eFD (trade reports) | Daily | 10 | None |
| Members' websites (press releases) | Daily | 10, shared by all members | None |
| Files imported by hand (FEC bulk files, Voteview, congress-legislators, portraits) | When you rerun their scripts | — | None |

**Delays built into the sources**
- **Stock trades:** members have up to 45 days to report a trade, so a trade can appear weeks after it happened.

APIs, with their keys, are in [.01_API_SOURCES_USED.md](.01_API_SOURCES_USED.md).

**How they are used:** the websites under "Read automatically" are read by the refresh and listed on Website updates in the app. Each item below is marked *(in use)*, *(by hand)* for files imported with a script, or *(not used yet)*.

## **Read automatically (see Website updates in the app)**
### Votes and committees
- **House Clerk roll calls** *(in use; House votes from 2023 on come through the Congress.gov API instead when its key is set)*: official House votes, with no key **(Federal only)**. [clerk.house.gov/Votes](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes)
- **Senate roll calls** *(in use)*: official Senate votes, with no key **(Federal only)**. [senate.gov roll call lists](https://www.senate.gov/legislative/LIS/roll_call_lists/vote_menu_119_2.htm)
- **Senate.gov and House Clerk member files** *(in use, compared daily with the reviewed committee list)*: the official current committee assignments for each chamber, with no key **(Federal only)**.
    - Senate: [senate.gov XML feeds](https://www.senate.gov/general/XML.htm)
    - House: [MemberData user guide](https://xml.house.gov/MemberData/MemberData_UserGuide.pdf)

### Statements
- **Members' press-release feeds** *(in use)*: the RSS or Atom feed on each sitting member's official website, found from the homepage or the address congress-legislators lists, with no key. Often the same message as the office's X and Facebook posts **(Federal only)**.

### Stock trades and disclosures
- **House Clerk disclosures** *(in use: the yearly filing index is read daily as the official record trades are matched against; the trade-report PDFs are read automatically (below))*: official yearly downloads, with trades as PDFs **(Federal only)**. [disclosures-clerk.house.gov/FinancialDisclosure](https://disclosures-clerk.house.gov/FinancialDisclosure)
- **House Clerk trade reports** *(in use, no key)*: each House member's electronically filed periodic transaction report (PDF), listed in the Clerk's yearly disclosure index and read automatically; reports filed on paper are scanned images, listed for review **(Federal only)**. [disclosures-clerk.house.gov](https://disclosures-clerk.house.gov/FinancialDisclosure)
- **Senate eFD** *(in use, no key)*: each senator's electronic trade reports, read from the official search site after agreeing to its terms (no commercial use other than news media, no credit ratings, no solicitation); paper reports are listed for review **(Federal only)**. [efdsearch.senate.gov](https://efdsearch.senate.gov/)

## **Imported by hand**
- **FEC bulk files** *(by hand)*: each researched group's money to each candidate since 2019, for the funder timelines (`scripts/build-funder-history.mjs`) **(Federal only)**. [fec.gov bulk data](https://www.fec.gov/data/browse-data/?tab=bulk-data)
- **Voteview** *(by hand)*: DW-NOMINATE ideology scores, copied into the research file and not refreshed **(Federal only)**. [voteview.com](https://voteview.com)
- **unitedstates/images** *(by hand)*: official public-domain congressional portraits (`scripts/build-portraits.py`). Candidates who never served in Congress use photos supplied for the site instead **(Federal only)**. [github.com/unitedstates/images](https://github.com/unitedstates/images)
- **Wikimedia Commons** *(by hand, for portraits)*: freely licensed photos for candidates without an official portrait, with no key. Check each photo's license and credit line before using it **(State and federal)**. [commons.wikimedia.org](https://commons.wikimedia.org/)
- **congress-legislators** *(by hand, scripts/build-ids.mjs)*: current committee assignments, each member's IDs across systems (Bioguide, FEC, Senate ID and others) for matching data, and each member's official social media accounts, so you know which feeds to follow **(Federal only)**.
    - Project: [github.com/unitedstates/congress-legislators](https://github.com/unitedstates/congress-legislators)
    - Current members file: [legislators-current.json](https://unitedstates.github.io/congress-legislators/legislators-current.json)
- **Senate eFD** *(holdings and older reports by hand, scripts/build-holdings.py, only with --accept-senate-terms; trade reports are read automatically, above)*: official search portal, no API. It asks every visitor to agree that reports won't be used for commercial purposes (other than news media), credit ratings or solicitation **(Federal only)**. [efdsearch.senate.gov](https://efdsearch.senate.gov/)
- **IRS political organization (527) data** *(not refreshed: a bulk download only, so 527 spenders link to the IRS search)*: bulk downloads of Forms 8871 and 8872 for groups that report to the IRS instead of the FEC, updated weekly, with no key **(Federal only)**. [IRS political organizations](https://www.irs.gov/node/471)

## **Not used yet**
- **Office of Government Ethics** *(not used yet)*: executive-branch financial disclosures, for candidates who held federal executive posts. No API, so by hand **(Federal only)**. [OGE Form 278e](https://oge.gov/web/278eGuide.nsf/Overview)
- **Interest group scorecards** *(not used yet: to be imported by hand once a year and shown as labeled context, never in a grade)*: each group's score for every member and the key votes it counted. Ask the ACLU and HRC for permission first; HRC's terms ask for it **(Federal only)**.
    - [Heritage Action](https://heritageaction.com/scorecard) · [AFL-CIO](https://aflcio.org/scorecard) · [LCV](https://www.lcv.org/congressional-scorecard/members-of-congress/) · [ACLU](https://www.aclu.org/congressional-scorecards) · [HRC](https://www.hrc.org/resources/congressional-scorecard)
- **Ballotpedia's state legislative scorecards** *(not used yet)*: a list of which groups rate state legislators in each state, used as a list of links **(State only)**. [ballotpedia.org/State_legislative_scorecards](https://ballotpedia.org/State_legislative_scorecards)
- **Congress Vote Tracker** *(not used yet: check who runs it and where its data comes from first)*. [congressvotetracker.org](https://congressvotetracker.org/scorecards.html)
- **Bill marking for Workers > Profit** *(not used yet; see [the Workers > Profit file](Scores/07_WORKERS_OVER_PROFIT_SCORE.md))*: who gains from a bill, and which side worker and business groups took **(Federal only)**.
    - Who gains: [CBO](https://www.cbo.gov/cost-estimates), [Joint Committee on Taxation](https://www.jct.gov/), [Tax Policy Center](https://www.taxpolicycenter.org/), [Penn Wharton Budget Model](https://budgetmodel.wharton.upenn.edu/)
    - Worker side: [Economic Policy Institute](https://www.epi.org/), [National Employment Law Project](https://www.nelp.org/)
    - Business side: [U.S. Chamber of Commerce](https://www.uschamber.com/how-they-voted), [NFIB](https://www.nfib.com/), [National Association of Manufacturers](https://www.nam.org/)
    - Tax bills, one from each side: [Tax Foundation](https://taxfoundation.org/), [ITEP](https://itep.org/)
    - White House position: [American Presidency Project](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/)

A few of these links I built from paths the sites list rather than opened directly: voteview.com, the congress-legislators project page and Wikimedia Commons. Where I wasn't sure of a group's exact scorecard page, I linked its home page. If one doesn't load, the site's home page will get you there.
